Thank you for using NoContact: Breakup Streak Log (Bundle ID: us.nocontactroast.streak). Below are answers to common questions and our support contact.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. Does NoContact block SMS messages, calls, or social media apps?

No. NoContact is a 100% offline breakup streak log, voluntary 60-second Cool-Down Lock timer, and grounding reflection tool. It helps you pause intentionally before reaching out without requesting system Screen Time, Messages, or contact permissions.

Q2. What is included in the Free version vs. NoContact Pro?

Free includes unlimited streak tracking, unlimited 60-second Cool-Down Locks, 5 daily SOS reality checks, Midnight Bestie and Calm Captain voices, preset Ex Codenames, and Midnight Plum 9:16 cards. NoContact Pro unlocks unlimited daily SOS checks, all 4 voices (adding Reality Coach and Boundary Boss), custom Ex Aliases, and all neon 9:16 poster skins.

Q3. How does the 60-Second Cool-Down Lock & 988 Safety Guard work?

Tap "Start 60s Cool-Down Lock Before Texting" in SOS Station to run a guided 60-second breathing pause. If an urge input ever includes self-harm, severe distress, or unsafe stalking/harassment language, Layer-0 SafetyCrisisGuard automatically silences all humor and displays immediate 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline and Crisis Text Line resources.

Q4. How do I restore my Pro purchase on another Apple device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID used for purchase, open the Station tab in NoContact, and tap "Restore Purchases."
