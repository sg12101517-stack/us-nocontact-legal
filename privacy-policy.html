Privacy Policy

App: NoContact: Breakup Streak Log (Bundle ID: us.nocontactroast.streak) · Last Updated: November 23, 2026

1. Zero Data Collection & 100% Offline Architecture

NoContact: Breakup Streak Log is engineered as an offline-only personal streak log, 60-second cool-down timer, and wake-up reflection deck. All impulse parsing, persona reality checks, streak timestamps, and Ex Codename settings run strictly on your device. We do not collect, transmit, rent, sell, or analyze any personal data. Specifically, the app never accesses or collects:

  • Names, email addresses, account credentials, contacts, or phone numbers
  • Your unsent impulse prompts, streak dates, checklist ticks, or custom Ex Aliases
  • SMS/iMessage content, call logs, third-party social media activity, location, camera, or photo library
  • Cloud AI APIs, third-party analytics SDKs, crash telemetry, or advertising identifiers (IDFA)

2. On-Device Storage & User-Initiated Exports

Your streak vault and deflected urge history stay exclusively inside your iPhone or iPad app sandbox. Uninstalling the app deletes all local app data. When you tap "Share 9:16 Card" or "Export Local Backup (JSON)", the resulting PNG or JSON file is shared solely through Apple's native iOS Share Sheet to the destination you choose.

3. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit 2)

Optional NoContact Pro plans (Lifetime, Annual with 3-day free trial, and Monthly) and "Restore Purchases" requests are processed exclusively by Apple Inc. via the App Store and StoreKit 2. We never receive or view your payment method, billing details, or Apple ID.

4. Support & Safety Links

If you tap an external crisis support link (such as 988 Lifeline, Crisis Text Line, or Love Is Respect) or our legal pages, Safari opens that public resource directly outside the app under its respective operator's privacy policy.
